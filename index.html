QA Crawler

A personal tool that collects QA job posts from public Telegram channels and delivers one digest email a day.

  • Reads posts from public Telegram channels only.
  • Sends a single digest email to its owner through the Gmail API.
  • Runs locally on the owner's computer — there are no external servers.
  • Not a public service: the tool is used by its author.
