Privacy policy

Application: QA Crawler · Last updated: 29 October 2026

QA Crawler is a personal, locally run tool. It collects job posts from public Telegram channels and emails a daily digest to its owner. This page explains what data the application touches.

What the application accesses

  • Public Telegram channels. Only posts that are already public and available without signing in. The application does not read private chats, contacts, or phone numbers.
  • Gmail, sending only. The application uses the gmail.send scope. It can send an email to the account owner and cannot read, modify, or delete mailbox contents.

What is stored, and where

  • An OAuth refresh token, stored only on the owner's computer in data/gmail_token.json.
  • A local SQLite database with already processed post IDs and texts, used to avoid duplicates: data/vacancies.db.

Nothing is uploaded to any server controlled by the application. There is no backend, no cloud storage, no analytics, and no advertising.

What is not done

  • No personal data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No mailbox contents are collected or stored.
  • No tracking or profiling of any kind.

Retention and deletion

All data lives on the owner's computer. To remove it, delete the data folder, or revoke the application's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
